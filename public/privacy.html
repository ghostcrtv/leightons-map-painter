Privacy

Leighton’s Map Painter · last updated 18 October 2026

The short version

Leighton’s Map Painter does not collect, send or sell any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What the game keeps, and where

  • Your worlds (painting, battles, custom countries, names) are saved only on your own device, in the browser’s storage (or the app’s storage).
  • Settings and badges (sounds, labels, which badges you’ve unlocked) are saved only on your device too.
  • Backup files and pictures are only made when you ask, and go wherever you choose to save or share them.

Deleting the app or clearing the website’s data removes everything. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Internet

The game only uses the internet to download itself and its maps and flags. After the first visit it can work offline. It never sends your game or any personal information anywhere.

Children

The game is made for kids and families. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to share with anyone.

Map data and flags

Borders come from Natural Earth (public domain). Flags come from the flag-icons project (MIT licence). Country ratings in the game are approximate game estimates, not official figures. See the third-party notices.
